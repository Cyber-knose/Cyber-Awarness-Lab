🛡️ Botnet Defense Strategies

  • ✔ Botnets are networks of compromised devices controlled by a C2 server
  • ✔ Common C2 protocols: IRC, HTTP(S), DNS tunneling, Tor
  • ✔ DDoS attacks overwhelm targets with traffic from thousands of bots
  • ✔ Network monitoring + DNS sinkholing can disrupt C2 channels
  • ✔ IoT devices are increasingly targeted for botnet recruitment
  • ✔ Use EDR to detect beaconing behavior (periodic callbacks to C2)
  • ✔ Block known malicious IPs and domains via threat intelligence feeds

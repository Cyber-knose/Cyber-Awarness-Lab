📱 SIM Swapping Attack Simulation

A SIM swap attack occurs when an attacker convinces a mobile carrier to transfer a victim's phone number to a SIM card in the attacker's possession, gaining access to SMS-based 2FA codes and account recovery messages.

🛡️ Protection

🛡️ How to Protect Against SIM Swapping:
  • 🔐 Use authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) instead of SMS 2FA
  • 🔐 Set a PIN/password on your mobile carrier account
  • 🔐 Use hardware security keys (FIDO2/U2F) for critical accounts
  • 🔐 Enable account recovery via email rather than phone number
  • 🔐 Use a separate "Google Voice" number for 2FA that's harder to swap
  • 🔐 Monitor for sudden loss of cellular service
  • 🔐 Contact carrier immediately if service drops unexpectedly
🚨 Signs Your SIM May Have Been Swapped:
  • 📱 Phone shows "No Service" but others in area have signal
  • 🔒 Unable to send/receive SMS or make calls
  • 📧 Receiving password reset emails you didn't request
  • 🏦 Unusual bank account activity or notifications

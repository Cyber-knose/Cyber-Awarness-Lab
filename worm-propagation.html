🛡️ How Worms Spread & How to Stop Them

  • ✔ Worms self-replicate without user interaction (unlike Trojans)
  • ✔ The 2017 WannaCry worm infected 230,000+ computers in one day
  • ✔ Patch management is the #1 defense against worms
  • ✔ Network segmentation limits lateral movement
  • ✔ EDR (Endpoint Detection & Response) tools detect worm behavior
  • ✔ Disable SMBv1, unused ports, and unnecessary services
